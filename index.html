How theAI draws

The AI never paints pixels. It plans ideas, then writes a tiny, simple SVG — which we inspect line by line before it reaches your screen.

01 · PLAN

AI picks ideas, fonts, colours

Your brief becomes 6 concept plans — names, reasons, an icon from 300+ open icons, one of 12 fonts and a 3-colour palette.

{ "name": "Rising Leaf",
  "kind": "custom",
  "font": "Fraunces",
  "palette": ["#3D6146",…] }
02 · DRAW

AI writes a simple SVG mark

For original concepts, the AI writes one flat, geometric mark: 2–3 colours, about 12 shapes, no text, no gradients.

<svg viewBox="0 0 100 100">
  <circle cx="50" cy="58" r="30" …/>
  <path d="M50 20 …"/>
</svg>
03 · CHECK

Every mark is cleaned and tested

We rebuild the SVG keeping only safe shapes and hex colours, then test its size, element count and position on the canvas.

✓ 9 shapes · 2.1 KB
✓ colours: hex only
✓ centred on canvas
FALLBACK If a mark fails twice, a template concept steps in with a small note — so you never see a broken logo.
